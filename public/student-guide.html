STUDENT GUIDE

Find and use your class material

Sign up

Enter your name, Gmail address, and a password. Read the school rules and privacy page. Check your email and confirm your address. Then sign in.

Find notes and exercises

Choose Notes or Exercise. Search by title or subject. You can filter by subject and date. Choose Open to read the post. Choose Download PDF to save a file.

Save a post

Choose Save on a post. The Saved filter is inside Notes and Exercise.

Change your details

Open Account to change your name, email, bio, or photo. Confirm a new email from your inbox. You can also change your password, download your data, or request account deletion.
