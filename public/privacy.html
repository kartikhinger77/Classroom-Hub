Privacy Policy

This draft explains what Classroom Hub stores. The school must review it with a qualified person before launch.

Information collected

We store your name, Gmail address, role, password hash, sign-in dates, sessions, profile bio and photo if you choose to add them, and the notes, exercises, saved posts, and account actions linked to your account. We do not ask for your address, phone number, or date of birth.

Why we use it

We use account details to provide access, protect accounts, send verification and password reset links, and keep a record of important admin actions.

Who can see it

You can see and edit your own account details. Teachers can create learning materials. Admins can see account email addresses and roles to manage the school service. Admins cannot see passwords, authenticator secrets, or session tokens.

How long we keep it

Sessions and notifications expire automatically. Trashed posts are removed after 30 days. Unverified accounts are removed after 7 days. When you request account deletion, the account is removed after a 7-day grace period; audit records are retained without the account identity.

Your choices

You can download your data or request account deletion in Account Center. Essential session cookies are used to keep you signed in. The site has no ads, tracking cookies, or third-party analytics.
