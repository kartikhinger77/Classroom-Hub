# Student guide

## Sign up

1. Open Classroom Hub and choose Create an account.
2. Enter your name, Gmail address, and a password.
3. Read and accept the Terms and Privacy Policy.
4. Open the email from Classroom Hub and confirm your address.
5. Sign in.

Students who are younger than the school's minimum age need a teacher or admin to create the account.

## Find learning material

Choose Notes or Exercise. Search by title or subject. Use the subject and date filters to narrow the list. Select Open to read a post or preview its PDF. Select Download PDF to save a copy.

Use Save to keep a post. The Saved filter is inside Notes and Exercise.

## Change your details

Open Account. You can edit your name, email, bio, and profile photo. Confirm a new email from the link we send. Use Change password to set a new password. You can review your sessions, download your data, or request account deletion.
