# Teacher guide

## Upload a PDF

1. Sign in and choose Notes or Exercise.
2. Select Create a post.
3. Enter the title, subject, and type.
4. Choose a PDF no larger than 10 MB.
5. Select Publish post.

The site accepts PDF files only. If the upload fails, check that the file opens as a PDF, is under 10 MB, and your connection is stable. Try again after the network recovers. Your post is private until you publish it.

## Write a post

1. Select Create a post.
2. Enter the title, subject, and type.
3. Write in the content box. Use the formatting buttons for bold, italic, lists, and quotes.
4. Select Publish post.

## Edit or delete

Open your post and choose Edit or Delete. Delete moves the post to Trash for 30 days. Only an admin can restore it from Trash. You can compare the current post with one of the last ten saved content and PDF versions, then restore that version.

## Account and support

Open Account to change your details or password and manage sessions. Contact your school's admin if you cannot sign in or your email is not verified.
